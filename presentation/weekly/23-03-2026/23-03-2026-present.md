### Key Observations
- **RAGTruth Baseline**: Shows near-zero "Retrieval" classification and CR coverage because the baseline generations contain no citations. The 0.999 rating is vacuous (default pass for non-retrieval sentences).
- **LettuceDetect**: Successfully injected 1,527 citations into sentences classified as requiring retrieval. The lower answer ratings (0.269 - 0.477) reflect active verification against the oracle context, providing a more meaningful measure of detector faithfulness.
